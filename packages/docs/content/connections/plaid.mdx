title: Plaid
description: Connect a bank through Plaid for agents to read balances and transactions.

Agents read the accounts of a bank you connect: what each holds or owes, and its transactions.
Plaid connects Personal Agent Proxy to the bank, in the US and Canada. Agents only ever read: they
can't move money or change anything at the bank. Every request goes to Plaid live; nothing is
copied.

## Before you start: Plaid keys

Hosted Personal Agent Proxy has its own Plaid keys, so skip to [Connect a bank](#connect-a-bank). A
self-hosted Personal Agent Proxy needs keys of its own, from a Plaid account you own. Without them,
Plaid is greyed out in the catalog.

<Steps>

### Make a Plaid account

Sign up at [dashboard.plaid.com](https://dashboard.plaid.com/signup). A new team in the US or Canada
starts on Plaid's free Trial plan, which connects up to 10 banks with real data. Removing a bank
doesn't free its place, so sign in to a bank again rather than disconnecting and connecting it.

### Copy the keys

On the dashboard's [Keys](https://dashboard.plaid.com/developers/keys) page, copy the client id and
the secret, and set them as `PLAID_CLIENT_ID` and `PLAID_SECRET`.

### Try it in Sandbox first

Sandbox has a secret of its own and connects only Plaid's test banks, with the username
`user_good` and the password `pass_good`. Set `PLAID_ENV` to `sandbox` with the Sandbox secret to
try it; leave it unset for real banks.

</Steps>

Plaid opens the sign-in of a bank that uses OAuth, such as Chase or Wells Fargo, in a popup, so
there is no redirect URI to register. Past the Trial, Plaid bills each connected bank monthly for
as long as it stays connected; disconnecting a bank in Personal Agent Proxy removes it at Plaid,
which ends that.

## Connect a bank

<Steps>

### Choose your bank

Open **Connections**, then **Add connection**, then **Plaid**, and **Choose your bank**. Plaid
opens over the page: find your bank, sign in to it and choose the accounts to share. Each bank
signed in to is a connection of its own, named by the bank.

### Review the defaults

A new bank gives agents nothing. Turn on Read balances and Read transactions for every agent on
the connection's page, or only for one agent on the agent's page.

</Steps>

## What agents can do

- **Read balances** lists the accounts with what each holds, or on a card or a loan what is owed,
  what is available, and a card's limit. Balances are as of Plaid's last update from the bank,
  several times a day.
- **Read transactions** lists each account's transactions, newest first, up to two years back from
  when the bank was connected, with the merchant, the amount, the date, Plaid's category and
  whether it is still pending. Amounts read as a statement does: money out negative, money in
  positive.

Plaid has no search in words: a search is a day, such as `2026-09-30`, for the transactions on it
and before it, or two days, `2026-09-01 2026-09-30`, for those between them. A search at the top is
in every account.

## When the bank asks to sign in again

Banks end a login now and then, such as after a password change. Agents are then told the bank
needs its login again. On the connection's page, **Sign in to the bank again** opens Plaid on the
same login, which keeps the connection and its permissions; it also changes which accounts are
shared.
