---
title: Information
description: Store addresses, payment cards and notes for agents to use.
---

Information is built in: details you want agents to use without connecting an outside service. It
has three collections:

- **Addresses**: a label, name, street, postal code, city and country.
- **Payment cards**: a label, cardholder, card number and expiry date.
- **Notes**: a title and free-form text.

<Steps>

### Add a record

Open **Information** in the sidebar and choose **Add** beside Addresses, Payment cards or Notes.

### Give agents access

Each collection has its own Read and Edit actions, so an agent can get your addresses without your
payment cards. Set them on the agent's page, which can also make a setting the default for every
agent. Edit also requires Read.

</Steps>
