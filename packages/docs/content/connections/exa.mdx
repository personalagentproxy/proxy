title: Exa
description: Connect Exa for agents to search the web and read web pages.
---

Agents search the web and read pages through Exa, on the Exa account you sign in with or the
API key you give. What they search for and the pages they read go to Exa, and searches count
toward that account's usage. Nothing is kept in Personal Agent Proxy beyond the activity log.

## Connect Exa

<Steps>

### Sign in, or use an API key

Open **Connections**, then **Add connection**. Choose **Exa** to sign in with your Exa account,
or the key beside it to paste an API key made at [dashboard.exa.ai](https://dashboard.exa.ai/api-keys). The key is checked with
Exa before the connection is made, and sent with every request as its bearer token.

Exa says nothing about who signed in, so a connection made with a key shows as the key's end,
and one signed in to as **Signed in**. Connect Exa as often as you like, for another account or
another key; rename a connection from the list of connections to tell them apart.

### Review the defaults

A new Exa connection gives every agent Search the web and Read web pages. Turn either off on the
connection's page, or for one agent on the agent's page.

</Steps>

To sign in again, or replace the key, open the connection: under its permissions, choose **Sign in
again** or **use an API key**. The connection keeps its name and every agent's settings.

## What agents can do

- **Search the web** takes what to find, described in plain words, and answers with the pages that
  fit, each with its title, address, publish date where known and the passages that match.
- **Read web pages** opens one page by its address and answers with its title and text, the first
  100,000 characters of it.

Agents can't change anything through Exa: both actions only read.
