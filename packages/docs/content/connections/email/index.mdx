title: Email

Personal Agent Proxy connects a mailbox with an app password: a password your mail provider makes
for one app, apart from the one you sign in with. Revoking it in your mail account disconnects
Personal Agent Proxy and nothing else. Emails stay with the provider; nothing is copied.

## What agents can do

Agents see the inbox, drafts and sent mail as one list. You can allow each action separately:

- **Read** lists, opens and searches email.
- **Mark read or unread**, **Flag**, **Archive** and **Move to Trash** act on messages in the
  inbox, and require Read. Trash can still be restored at the provider.
- **Write drafts** creates, edits and deletes drafts, and requires Read. Received messages are
  never edited or deleted for good.
- **Send** sends a new email or a saved draft. It does not require Read, so an agent can send
  without seeing the mailbox.

## Connect a mailbox

<Steps>

### Make an app password

Follow the steps for your provider: [Gmail](/connections/email/gmail),
[iCloud](/connections/email/icloud), [Fastmail](/connections/email/fastmail),
[Yahoo](/connections/email/yahoo) or [any other](/connections/email/other).

### Connect the mailbox

Open **Connections**, then **Add connection**, and **Connect** on Email. Pick the provider, then
enter the mailbox's address and the app password.

### Give agents access

A new mailbox gives agents no access. On the connection's page, turn on the actions every agent
gets by default, or set them for one agent on the agent's page.

</Steps>

## When it does not connect

**"The mailbox turned the password down."** Check that the address is the mailbox the password
was made for, or make a new app password.

**"The mail server could not be reached."** With Other, check the servers and the SMTP port. On
your own server, see [Troubleshooting](/self-hosting/troubleshooting#a-mailbox-does-not-connect).
