title: Fastmail
description: Make an app password for a Fastmail mailbox.
---

<Steps>

### Create the app password

In Fastmail, open **Settings → Privacy & Security** and create a **New app password** with access
to mail over IMAP and SMTP.

### Connect the mailbox

[Connect it](/connections/email#connect-the-mailbox) with **Fastmail**.

</Steps>
