---
title: Gmail
description: Make an app password for a Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox.
---

<Steps>

### Turn on 2-Step Verification

Google only makes app passwords for accounts with
[2-Step Verification](https://myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/twosv) on.

### Create the app password

Open [App passwords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords), check the account in the top
right corner, enter a name such as Personal Agent Proxy, and **Create**. Google shows the password
once.

### Connect the mailbox

[Connect it](/connections/email#connect-the-mailbox) with **Gmail or Google Workspace**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Changing your Google password revokes every app password, which disconnects Personal Agent
  Proxy. Make a new one and connect the mailbox again.
</Callout>

## "The setting you are looking for is not available for your account"

Google shows this on the App passwords page when the account cannot make one:

- **2-Step Verification is off.** Turn it on, then open the page again.
- **2-Step Verification uses only security keys.** Add another way to verify, such as an
  authenticator app or your phone.
- **Advanced Protection is on.** Accounts in Google's Advanced Protection Program cannot use app
  passwords.
- **It is a work or school account.** Your Google Workspace admin decides; see below.

## Google Workspace

For a work or school account, the admin sets these in the
[Google Admin console](https://admin.google.com):

- Under **Security → Authentication → 2-Step Verification**, people need to be allowed to turn
  on 2-Step Verification, and its methods cannot be limited to security keys only.
- Under **Apps → Google Workspace → Gmail → End User Access**, IMAP access needs to be on.

If your admin does not allow app passwords, the mailbox cannot be connected yet.
