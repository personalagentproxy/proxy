---
title: iCloud
description: Make an app-specific password for an iCloud Mail mailbox.
---

<Steps>

### Turn on two-factor authentication

Apple only makes app-specific passwords for Apple Accounts with two-factor authentication. iCloud
Mail also needs to be on in iCloud settings on one of your devices.

### Create the app password

Sign in at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/account/manage), open **Sign-In and
Security**, then **App-Specific Passwords**, and add one named Personal Agent Proxy.

### Connect the mailbox

[Connect it](/connections/email#connect-the-mailbox) with **iCloud** and your iCloud Mail address,
such as `name@icloud.com`.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Changing your Apple Account password revokes app-specific passwords, which disconnects Personal
  Agent Proxy. Make a new one and connect the mailbox again.
</Callout>
