---
title: Vault
description: Keep what your agents should know about you, and let them remember what they learn.
---

The vault is built in: what you want every agent to know about you, without connecting an outside
service. It is also your agents' memory: an agent you let edit it can keep what it learns, such as
how you like things done, so the next agent doesn't have to ask.

You have one of your own in every organization you are in, which nobody else there sees and which
can't be shared. For what more than your own agents should know, such as the office address or how
a team writes, make another: a vault you name and share as any connection, with everyone in the
organization or with members by name ([Organizations](/organizations)). Your own agents reach
yours and every vault shared with you; a shared agent reaches the ones shared with everyone alone.

Each entry is a title, a type and its content, such as "Home", Address and the address written
out. The types are Text, Address, Phone number, Email, Link and Date. Agents read every entry as
written.

<Steps>

### Add an entry

Open **Connections** in the sidebar, then **My vault**, first in the list, and choose **Add
entry** on its **Entries** tab. That is your own vault; a vault someone made is listed by its
name, and opens the same way.

### Make a vault to share

Choose **Vault** under **Add connection** and name it: its name is what tells agents what belongs
in it. It is listed under **Connections** by that name, and its page has its entries, its
permissions and **Sharing**: add the members it is for, or share it with everyone in the
organization. Whoever it is shared with reads and edits its entries as you do. Renaming it, or
deleting it with its entries, is yours alone, or the organization's owner's too once everyone has
it.

### Choose what agents can do

A vault has two actions, **Read entries** and **Edit entries**, which also requires Read. Every
agent gets both by default. Turn either off for one agent on the agent's page, or for every agent
there as the default.

</Steps>

## Agents and the vault

An agent that can read the vault is told to look there before asking you something about yourself.
One that can also edit it is told to save what would help next time as an entry, and to update an
entry when something has changed rather than add another. An agent with more than one vault is
told which is yours and what the others are called, to keep what is about you alone in yours and
the rest in the vault named for it. Every
entry an agent reads, adds or edits is in the activity log.
