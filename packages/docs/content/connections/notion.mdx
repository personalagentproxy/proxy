title: Notion
description: Connect a Notion workspace for agents to read, create and edit pages.
---

Agents work with the pages and databases the person who connects Notion can reach in the workspace
they choose. Pages stay in Notion; nothing is copied.

## Connect Notion

<Steps>

### Sign in with Notion

Open **Connections**, then **Add connection**, and **Sign in with Notion**. Choose a workspace and
approve access. Connecting another workspace makes another connection.

### Review the defaults

A new Notion connection gives every agent Read pages. Create pages and Edit pages are off by
default. Turn them on for every agent on the connection's page, or only for one agent on the
agent's page.

</Steps>

## What agents can do

Notion appears as a tree of pages, databases, saved database views and rows. Agents can:

- **Read pages** lists, searches and opens pages and databases, including database rows.
- **Create pages** adds a private page at the top of the workspace, a page inside another page or a
  row inside a database.
- **Edit pages** changes a page's title, properties and content. It requires Read pages.

Databases and saved views themselves cannot be edited. Personal Agent Proxy never deletes Notion
pages because Notion's MCP server does not provide a tool for it.

<Callout>

A Notion connection lasts at most 180 days, even while its tokens are refreshed. Sign in again
after Notion expires it.

</Callout>
