---
title: Keenable
description: Connect Keenable for agents to search the web and read web pages.
---

Agents search the web and read pages through Keenable, on the Keenable account you sign in with or the
API key you give. What they search for and the pages they read go to Keenable, and searches count
toward that account's usage. Nothing is kept in Personal Agent Proxy beyond the activity log.

## Connect Keenable

<Steps>

### Sign in, or use an API key

Open **Connections**, then **Add connection**. Choose **Keenable** to sign in with your Keenable account,
or the key beside it to paste an API key made at [app.keenable.ai](https://app.keenable.ai/console). The key is checked with
Keenable before the connection is made, and sent with every request as its bearer token.

Keenable says nothing about who signed in, so a connection made with a key shows as the key's end,
and one signed in to as **Signed in**. Connect Keenable as often as you like, for another account or
another key; rename a connection from the list of connections to tell them apart.

### Review the defaults

A new Keenable connection gives every agent Search the web and Read web pages. Turn either off on the
connection's page, or for one agent on the agent's page.

</Steps>

To sign in again, or replace the key, open the connection: under its permissions, choose **Sign in
again** or **use an API key**. The connection keeps its name and every agent's settings.

## What agents can do

- **Search the web** takes what to find, described in plain words, and answers with the pages that
  fit, each with its title, address, publish date where known and the text that matches.
- **Read web pages** opens one page by its address and answers with its title and text as Markdown,
  the first 100,000 characters of it, followed by a note where the page goes on. A page that can't
  be read fails, saying why and what to try next: nothing at the address, the site turned the
  fetcher away, no text to read, or the site out of reach or busy for now.

Agents can't change anything through Keenable: both actions only read.
