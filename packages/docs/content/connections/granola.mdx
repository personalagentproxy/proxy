---
title: Granola
description: Connect Granola meeting notes and transcripts for agents to read.
---

Agents can read the meeting notes and transcripts in your Granola account. Meetings stay in
Granola; nothing is copied.

## Connect Granola

<Steps>

### Sign in with Granola

Open **Connections**, then **Add connection**, and **Sign in with Granola**. Once you approve
access, Granola sends you back to the new connection.

### Review the defaults

A new Granola connection gives every agent Read notes and Read transcripts. Turn either off on the
connection's page, or for one agent on the agent's page.

</Steps>

## What agents can read

Granola is read-only: agents cannot change your meetings. Its two actions are independent:

- **Read notes** opens a meeting's title, date, attendees, link, summary and your private notes.
- **Read transcripts** opens the conversation word for word.
